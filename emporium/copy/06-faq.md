## What is all this?

Most days serve you whatever's on the menu. Emails, errands, the same route, the same three thoughts on repeat. Some of it is perfectly good. A fair bit of it is the mental version of junk food: it fills you up, leaves you oddly hungry, and somehow you keep going back for seconds. (So do I. I'm not judging. I sell spices, not diets.)

The Emporium doesn't take anything off your plate. It hands you one small pinch to add. A different way home. A thank-you you didn't plan. A cloud that looks like a slightly suspicious potato. You put it into your day, and the day tastes a little more like yours.

That's all. It's gloriously unnecessary, and that's rather the point.

## Why is there no daily must-do?

Because you have plenty of those already.

The shop is always open. No closing time, no streak, no little flame that goes out if you miss a day. Come in when you feel like it. Skip a day, or a week, or a month. The shelves will still be here, and so will I.

If the first pinch doesn't suit you, ask for another. You get three. After the third, the pan on the scales stays empty until tomorrow. Not as a punishment: it just means today your spice will come from somewhere else. Nobody leaves this shop empty-handed.

And if you take a pinch and your first thought is "Sophie, honestly, not today"... then not today. Not everything uncomfortable is growth, and not everything comfortable is laziness. :)

## What do the flavours mean?

Every pinch belongs to a flavour. You don't pick it: the Emporium does, the way a good shopkeeper slips something into your bag that you didn't know you needed.

Salt for when you need your feet back on the ground. Sweet for when you could do with some kindness, including your own. Sour for when you're feeling a bit cheeky. Bitter for the thing you keep walking past. Spice for a little courage. And a new pan, which isn't a flavour at all, for when the trouble is the same old pan.

You find out which one you got when the pinch lands on the counter. (The shop is surprisingly good at this.)

## What does it cost?

Nothing. The pinches are free, and they'll stay free.

If you'd like a gentle reminder in your inbox each morning, that's free too (it's at the bottom of the page, next to the sleeping cat). One day there may be something on the shelf to take home, made just for you. When there is, I'll tell you. Until then, help yourself.
