# 03 · The counter (today's pinch)

- **Status:** DRAFT, waiting for Sophie
- **Slot:** `#counter`: the cream card on the counter, the button under it, and the small way back.
- **The pinch texts themselves:** `01-missions-en.md` (No. 01–57).

---

## The card

**Eyebrow** (above the pinch): `{Flavour} · No. {nn}`, for example *Sour · No. 22*

**Before the first pinch** (card empty):
> Today's pinch is waiting. Choose a flavour, or let the shop choose for you.

**Same day, back again** (the card shows the same pinch):
> Here's today's pinch again. It kept.

**If the visitor came from the morning email and we know their name** (the shopkeeper looks up once):
> Morning, {Name}. Here's the counter.

## The buttons

- First: **TAKE A PINCH**
- Second and third: **ONE MORE PINCH**
- After the third, the button goes and this takes its place:

> **That's your three.**
> The pan on the scales is empty for today. Your spice will come from somewhere else now. Keep your eyes open, and come back tomorrow.

## The way back (already on the page)

> First time in? Start at the door.
