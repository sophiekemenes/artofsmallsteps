# 04 · The six flavour stories

- **Status:** DRAFT, waiting for Sophie
- **Slot:** the dashed box next to each shelf, under the name and the one-line meaning (those stay as they are).
- **Rule:** each story picks up at least one object that is really on that shelf's picture, so the words and the picture meet.

---

## Salt · grounding, the basics put right

Salt doesn't show off. You only notice it when it's missing. These pinches are for days that have come a bit unstitched: one drawer put in order, three sounds you actually listen to, a stretch before the next thing. Plain, small, and oddly steadying. Like a mended sock. (Yes, people still mend socks. With a darning mushroom, no less. I checked.)

## Sweet · kindness, something you give yourself

Sweet is kindness, and some of it is for other people: a thank-you you didn't plan, a door held, a song passed on. But a fair share of it is for you. Five minutes that don't have to be useful. A biscuit eaten slowly, on purpose. You're allowed to be on your own list.

## Sour · mischief, cheek, a little impudence

This is the cheeky shelf. A jar of keys that open nothing, a catapult, a rubber stamp for approving things nobody asked you to approve. The pinches here are small acts of mischief: odd socks, a secret note, narrating the laundry like a wildlife film. Nobody gets hurt. Somebody, probably you, grins.

## Bitter · what you keep walking past

The envelope you haven't opened. The mirror turned face down. The "one day" that never quite gets a date. These pinches don't ask you to fix anything. They ask you to look, for a minute, at the thing you've been politely ignoring. Bitter is an acquired taste. So is honesty. (The cat hanging off this shelf has opinions about both.)

## Spice · courage, and a bit of risk

A message after a long silence. Five minutes of something you're a complete beginner at. Saying the nice thing out loud instead of only thinking it. None of it is a mountain. It's more like the first bite of a chilli: your face does something alarming, and then you're glad you did.

## A new pan · a change of tool, or of place

Sometimes the recipe is fine and it's the pan that's tired. These pinches change the tool or the place: a different route, a book picked for its title, an ordinary object studied like a visitor from another planet. Same you, same day, new pan. (The black cat asleep in this one would like you to keep your voice down.)
