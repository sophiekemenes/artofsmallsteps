## The note

Hello. It's me, the one behind the counter.

I made this shop for the ordinary days. Not the bad ones, not the brilliant ones, just the ones that turn up and get served, like Tuesdays. On those days nobody needs a five-step plan or a new life. One small thing to put in is plenty.

So that's what I keep on the counter. Take a pinch, put it in, see what it does. And if it does nothing at all, that's fine too. There's always tomorrow, and the shop doesn't close.

With love,
Sophie
