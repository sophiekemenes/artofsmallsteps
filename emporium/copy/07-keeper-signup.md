## The note

Hello, hon. It's me, the one behind the counter.

I love this shop. I made it for the ordinary days, the ones that are sometimes almost criminally average. I know I've got enough imagination and playfulness to rattle happily along through life. But some days, just getting the existing things done takes so much out of me that I need a little extra spice from outside. And if nobody else was going to make it for me, I decided I'd make it myself. And if you're here, for you too.

There are days when nobody needs a five-step plan, or a whole new life. All you need is one itsy-bitsy little thing to put in, and that's plenty.

So that's what's on my counter right now. Just take a pinch, drop it into your day, and see what it does. If it happens to do nothing at all, that's perfectly fine too. Because there's always tomorrow, and thank heavens (and the genies of the www, and the great superhighways of the internet) this shop never closes. It'll be here when you need it. And it'll be here for me too: you might as well know that I come back regularly myself, for a pinch of randomly generated goodness.

With love,
Sophie
