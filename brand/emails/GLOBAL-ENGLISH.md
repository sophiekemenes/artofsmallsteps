# Global English: house rule for all RECHART / Art of Small Steps copy

Decided by Sophie 2026-11-19. The UK comes first, but the same copy will later sell in the US and to English speakers across Europe, many of them non-native. So write it once, for everyone:

- **Spelling stays British** (colour, favourite, grey, organise). Everyone reads it, and it's the brand's home.
- **No words that mean something else, or nothing, outside the UK.** Use the version everyone understands:
  - vest → tank top · torch → a little light / flashlight · lift → elevator · rubbish → trash, or "empty the bin"
  - loo → bathroom · trainers → running shoes · secondary school → high school · marks → grades
  - satnav → GPS · broadband provider → internet provider · holiday house → summer house
- **No UK-only slang or in-jokes:** "fancy" (= like), "wind someone up", "End of.", "Gran", "lads", "takeaway", Benidorm as a wink.
- **Food and places:** choose things known worldwide (lasagne, bread roll) over British ones (shepherd's pie, cheese scone).
- **Idioms are fine when a non-native can work them out from the words** ("the penny dropped", "knock things into shape", "turn on the waterworks").
- **Seasonal UK-only material** (dates, UK holidays) is fine where the email itself is UK-only, but keep the wording general.
