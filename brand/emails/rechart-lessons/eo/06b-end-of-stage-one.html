We've reached the end of the first stage. The loosening-up, warming-up part.

By now, together, we've woken up:

  • how much power the downstairs neighbour, your subconscious, has over your abilities, your feelings and your motivation
  • how the words you use can change the way you act
  • how useful the filters are that you see your own world through
  • how writing by hand can soothe, calm you down, and sometimes even heal a little

In other words: your inner navigator is awake. Not perfect, not finished (nobody's navigator is ever finished, that's rather the point), but awake, curious, and paying attention.

So now we can do what every navigator does before a long voyage.

We draw the map.

Not just any map. Your other map. The one where the routes are yours, the destinations are yours, and the reasons you set off are yours too.

And I mean that quite literally. From the next lesson on, you'll be drawing it, by hand, on one big sheet of paper. A3 if you have it, or two ordinary sheets taped together. Every lesson adds one layer: a compass, a home port, a lighthouse on the horizon, a few islands, and at the end, an X or two, marking where you're headed.

You don't need to be able to draw. My own maps would make a real cartographer weep quietly into their sextant. It's not about being pretty. It's about being yours.

That's Stage 2 · Draw Your Other Map.

And after it comes Stage 3 · Stormproof Your Vessel. Because a map is lovely, but out at sea there's weather. In the third stage we look at the fears that tend to show up once you've actually chosen a direction, at the reefs and the supplies, and at how to step aboard as the person who's already on the way.

A few things you'll want close by:

  • your notebook, and your pen (yes, that pen)
  • your coloured pens or pencils
  • a few envelopes
  • that one big sheet of paper, for the map

What to expect by the end: clear goals, reachable and measurable, that actually agree with your inner wishes and with the bigger purpose you have in the world. Laid out in front of you on paper, plain to see.

And, alongside them, playful, funny or thought-provoking tasks, so you can get moving when you need to, so your subconscious helps rather than gets in the way, and so you can handle doubts and fears in their proper place.
