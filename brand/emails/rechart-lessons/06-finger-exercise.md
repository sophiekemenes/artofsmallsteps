**Subject:** Lesson 6: A starry night, and a secret you can carry in your hand

**Preview text:** One feeling from your past, anchored to your fingertips. Available any time.

Hi!

If you made it to the end of last time's exercise, you now have a sheet of paper in front of you with a whole lot of inner resources, all linked to the goals you've just poured out of yourself.

Please choose three of them! The three you think are the most important of all. The ones you'd like to be able to call up any time.

Choose three that come with memories of your own. Something that has already happened to you, something you've lived through once. Feel free to scan through your past, and here it doesn't really matter how "big" a success you were in the middle of. What matters is how you felt.

Let me tell you about a very, very old experience of mine, one of the first I ever anchored. Maybe it'll help you see what we're looking for.

Not long after secondary school, a group of friends and I spent a week at one of the girls' holiday house in Vác, a little town on the Danube just north of Budapest. Those teenage summers are always seen through a sort of golden filter, once enough time has passed :)

We had everything a party needs: home-brewed Curaçao (blue food colouring and vodka) (brrr) (everyone goes through stuff like this), a guitar, a cauldron of stew over the fire, young people, a campfire.

It was a beautiful, warm August night, full of shooting stars. The air was balmy, our tanning skin was still giving off the heat of the day, but cool, pleasant breezes were blowing too.

I wandered off from the others a little, up to a higher spot on the plot, just because. Maybe to watch the shooting stars for a bit. Down below, my friend Andrew's full-lunged whoop was echoing between the hills, I looked up at the sky, and suddenly… suddenly everything was "in its place".

The people, the world, the universe, the feel of the grass under my sunburnt calves, the touch of my vest on my shoulder, and the way my own hair tickled my neck.

A calm came over me, and a faith, not in anything, not about anything, just in itself. I wasn't thinking about anything, I was just present, and everything else was present too, and that was how it should be.

It was a wonderful feeling. A kind of otherworldly calm, and trust that things would be all right, however they turned out…

Which, of course, as the years went by, I completely forgot, until I went looking in my past for resources on purpose, and rediscovered this little pearl in my life.

When it comes to naming the resource, I can't be all that precise, but I've forgiven myself for that. It doesn't matter if your resource has no dictionary word. Let it be intense, let it be all-pervading, and know that it will help you if you can live it again any time.

And I know for certain that you have things like this too, countless ones.

Maybe tucked away in one of the downstairs neighbour's cupboards, and if you ask nicely, it might just bring it out for you.

We work with only one experience like this at a time, so search your memories, use the list, or anything you've written during the exercises, find it, and choose which one it'll be!

By the time we've finished, this emotional and mental state, this inner resource, will be available to you any time.

## Finger exercise

Most NLP exercises are done in pairs, so to make it easier, I've made you an audio recording where I guide you through the anchoring. You can download it here: [ANCHORING-AUDIO-LINK]

If you'd rather do it consciously, just read on and go step by step!

**a)** Sit down on a chair and make yourself comfortable, or stand in a posture you choose, one you feel suits your chosen resource better.

**b)** Think about where you'll anchor it. What will the touch be? Most of the time the handiest is the hand (ha ha), and if you've never done anything like this before, I suggest a fairly hidden movement: say, touching the index finger and thumb of your left hand together. Of course you can choose anything, but let it be a movement you never or rarely make by accident, and which is memorable to you for exactly that reason.

**c) Calling the feeling back**

Take a deep breath, and slowly let it out. You can close your eyes if that's more comfortable… and slowly let that old memory come to you, the one where this resource was intensely yours.

Go at your own pace, and slowly, gently, take full possession of the experience.

See what you saw then.

Hear what you heard then.

Let the feeling fill you up inside, slowly but surely. Quietly at first… then more and more strongly.

When the experience is getting close to its peak, touch your fingers together, or make your chosen movement, and hold them there until you're almost vibrating with the experience you're reliving! When it's at its very height, open your eyes, step out of it, and end the touch too.

**d)** Would you please count backwards from a hundred in twos? And when you get to eighty, in threes? Then please take the rubbish out, feed the dog, or do a bit of washing up.

This is the procedure for jolting yourself out of it, and it's very important too. Here in this letter, that's the dog. :)

**e) Test**

When you're more or less your everyday self again, it's time for the test. Curiously, and without preparing for it, touch your fingers together, or make the movement you chose. Look inwards, and notice whether the feeling you anchored comes back to you. It usually starts gently, and the longer you hold the movement, the more intense it gets.

Well, did it work?

If not, have another go!
Take care with how clear the experience and the memory are!
Take care with how intense it is!
These are important ingredients.
Repeat it three times if you need to.

If it did, CONGRATULATIONS :)

Now you have a secret thing you can reach for any time you feel your surroundings aren't helping you call up your inner values.

I asked you for three resources like this, didn't I? How about anchoring them as a bouquet?

Put one on your index finger, one on your middle finger and one on your ring finger, since you can touch all three to your thumb at once.

Or however feels good to you, however you like!

You'll feel the rhythm of it. Let your intuition lead you.

How did you like it?
