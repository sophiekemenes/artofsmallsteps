Hi {{Nickname}},

The journey officially starts on 3 January, but one stage is already open, and it's yours: **Find Your Bearings.**

It's a short visualisation game. I talk, you close your eyes, and a few pictures turn up in your head: a cube, a ladder, a lion, and a few more. You write down what you saw. Then you open the key and look at what those pictures might be telling you about where you stand right now.

I first recorded it in 2016, and it still surprises me. (Including when I play it myself. Which I do, from time to time, purely for research. Obviously.)

What you'll need: about five quiet minutes, a pen, and a bit of trust in the first picture that shows up. Don't edit it. The first one is the honest one.

**[Find your bearings →](https://artofsmallsteps.com/wayfinder-method/find-your-bearings/)**

One tip: play it before you read the key. It works much better that way round.

**If you've already played it** (some of you found it through the message in a bottle before booking): lovely, you're one step ahead. You don't have to do it again. But if you're curious, play it once more and see whether any of the pictures have changed. Sometimes the sea is a different colour a few weeks later.
