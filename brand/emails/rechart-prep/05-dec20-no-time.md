**Subject:** The most honest lie of December

**Preview text:** "I haven't got time." A small sentence to keep in your pocket for January.

---

Hi {{Nickname}},

It's the Sunday before Christmas, so I'll guess you haven't got much time. (I'll also guess you're reading this anyway, in a queue, or hiding in the bathroom for five minutes of peace. Hello. Your secret is safe with me.)

Which brings me to a sentence I've been poking at for years: *I haven't got time.*

It's a brilliant sentence. Rock solid. You say it, throw your hands up, and nobody can argue. Those pesky circumstances.

But here's a little mind game. I promise it won't hurt, and if not an "aha", you'll at least get a "hmm".

When you say "I haven't got time for it", what you're actually saying is: *right now, this isn't a priority for me.* Other things go further up the list, this one goes further down, and whoops, the day's run out.

Try it with something you keep not doing. Swap in the deeper reason behind it:

"I haven't got time to exercise" = "Right now, my health isn't a priority."

The first time I did this, gigantic pennies came raining down from the sky. Excuse me? Hang on a minute. Of course it's a priority!

And sometimes, honestly, it isn't. Not right now. That's fine too. Then you can let the guilt go and say, kindly: *sorry, you didn't make the top five this time.* That's not failure. That's choosing.

Here's why I'm telling you this five days before Christmas.

From 3 January, RECHART is going to ask for some of your time. Sometimes more, sometimes less. And at some point in January, probably on a grey Tuesday, a little voice will say: *I haven't got time for this.*

So before that voice turns up, a small favour, if you're up for it: write down why you booked. Not the sensible reason. The real one. What's the bigger thing this journey stands for, for you? Put it on a sticky note, in your phone, on the first page of a notebook, wherever you'll find it again.

Then, in January, when we're already gliding over the waves and that little voice pipes up, you'll have your answer within reach.

Have a gentle Christmas. Make time for the things that make the top five.

Sophie
