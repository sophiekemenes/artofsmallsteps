# Find Your Bearings — transcript

## Taking your bearings

Before a navigator sets off anywhere, they do one small thing first. They take their bearings: where am I standing, right now?

That's what we're going to do together. Only, this time, the map is inside you.

Keep a pen and paper close. You can pause the video at any point to write your answers down, and you can always come back to it later.

Turn your imagination right up, be honest, and always go with your very first impression.

Don't worry, there are no wrong answers here. Only symbols hiding deep in your mind, and at the end, I'll help you read them.

Ready? Let's go.

## The cube

Imagine you're standing in the middle of a vast, beautiful desert.

Your eyes rest on the soft curves of the dunes. Near and far, there's nothing at all…

…except a cube, which catches your eye.

Here's your first task. Tell me, what is this cube like?

What colour is it? How big? What is it made of? Where exactly is it?

What are its edges like? And its surface? Have a really good look.

*(Pause and write it down.)*

## The ladder

Not long after, you discover a ladder.

What is this ladder like? What's it made of? Is it a single ladder, or a stepladder?

Does it look old, or new? Does it stand up on its own, or does it have to lean against something?

Would you dare to step onto it?

If, in the pictures in your head, you reach out and touch these things, that's fine too. Let your imaginary self move around freely.

*(Pause and write it down.)*

## The lion

Then, all of a sudden, you hear a growl from behind the nearest dune.

A lion appears, and it's quite clear it's about to attack.

Quick, what do you do?

*(Pause and write it down.)*

Written it down? Good. Then here's the good news.

Maybe it's the heat, maybe something else, but before anything can happen, the lion simply dissolves into thin air. You must have imagined it.

## The road

Carry on, because soon you'll come across a road.

Use your mind's eye again. What is this road like? Take a good look.

Is it straight, or does it wind? Does it look as if people walk it often? What might it be for?

Is it paved? Notice everything.

*(Pause and write it down.)*

## The sea

Set off along it. And once you have, before long you'll hear gulls crying in the distance…

…and it doesn't take much more before you come out at the sea.

Take a deep breath, and look around. Write down what you see.

What's the weather like? What's the water like: calm and quiet, or rough? Clear, or murky?

And the shore: is it sand, pebbles, or rock?

Let yourself notice everything.

*(Pause and write it down.)*

## The house and the sign

In the distance, you spot a house. You set off towards it…

…but then you notice a sign, too. Hmm. Is there anything written on it?

If there isn't, let it be. Just keep walking.

Start looking at the house, because you can make out more and more of it.

Tell me, what is it like? What is it built from? How does it make you feel?

Does anyone live there? Gather up everything that comes to you.

*(Pause and write it down.)*

## The horse

And finally, just before you reach the house, a horse appears.

What is it like? What does it do when it sees you?

And what does it do after that? Write everything down.

*(Pause and write it down.)*
