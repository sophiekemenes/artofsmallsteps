# Art of Small Steps — Visual Direction (v1, chosen)

Status: **Direction A ("King's Cross Light") chosen by Sophie**, 2026-09-25, with Direction C ("River Stone") noted as a secondary textural influence. Explored via an interactive moodboard artifact with 4 directions (A: King's Cross Light, B: Still Morning, C: River Stone, D: Paper & Ink) — B and D archived as alternatives, not discarded permanently.

## Chosen palette — "King's Cross Light"

| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Near-white | `#F7F5F0` | Page background |
| Buttery cream | `#EFE9DA` | Secondary surface |
| Silver-grey | `#C7CAD0` | Cool neutral, dividers |
| Warm grey | `#8B8A85` | Muted text / secondary |
| Copper accent | `#B87745` | Single warm accent — sparingly |

Mood: veiled white, cream and silver-grey, one warm copper accent. A threshold feeling — the space between two states — very soft, almost bodiless light that is nevertheless not cold.

## Secondary influence — "River Stone" (Direction C)

Slate blue-grey, moss, pale stone. Considered as a possible *textural* layer under the King's Cross palette (e.g. matte/stone-like surface treatment) to avoid the palette reading as too glossy/digital — not a competing direction, a possible material quality added to A. Not yet decided.

## Resolved — the King's Cross reference itself

**Aesthetic-only, decided by Sophie (2026-09-25).** The light and palette quality is what's wanted; the death/afterlife narrative behind the scene is not carried forward into the brand at all — no "threshold"/liminal-space motif in copy, imagery, or motion. The literary origin of the palette stays private background, not brand material.

## Discarded-for-now directions

- **B — Still Morning**: sand, muted terracotta, sage, warm charcoal. More grounded/human, less ethereal. Kept as reference if A ever reads as too otherworldly for a given context (e.g. transactional product pages).
- **D — Paper & Ink**: paper tone, graphite, rust accent. Tied functionally to the Notebook + Pen ritual objects from the Újratervezés source material. Worth revisiting specifically for any physical/printable materials (workbook exports, PDF lesson sheets) even if not the primary digital brand palette.

## Illustration layer — "Harbor Light" (chosen, 2026-11-01)

Added for the RECHART / Wayfinder Method product line specifically (sea, sunset, navigation imagery), confirmed by Sophie after iterating on ChatGPT-generated samples. Sourced partly from Kincsestérkép's own existing content colors (verified from the live site: `#142230`, `#1C4F6B`, `#F3F6F9`, `#BFD9E8` already in use there) and partly from its existing sunset/sea photography (`eletkaland-hero.jpg`, the brass-compass photo).

| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight contour | `#142230` | Ink/outline color for all illustrated linework |
| Deep sea blue | `#1C4F6B` | Primary blue |
| Dusty light blue | `#7FA8C9` | Secondary / wash blue |
| Warm cream paper | `#F7F5F0` | Background — **not** icy white; must match the main site background, not a separate cold white |
| Copper | `#B87345` | Metal accent — near-identical to the King's Cross copper (`#B87745`), confirming the two directions share a real accent color |

**Copper ratio: tuned per image, not a fixed rule.** An early ChatGPT self-description proposed capping copper at 5–10%; Sophie explicitly rejected that as a fixed rule — some images (e.g. a compass where the object itself is brass) should let copper dominate because it reads well. Judge each image on its own, don't enforce a percentage.

**Two-tier system, not one asset type:**
1. **Hero / photographic imagery** — stays closer to photoreal but leans more watercolor than the early exploratory prompts did: visible watercolor texture and ink linework painted across the *whole* image, not just the edges. Warm cream paper base, not icy white.
2. **Illustrated icons / section dividers / email headers / list bullets** — fully illustrated watercolor-and-ink style (no photorealism at all), used for the RECHART/Wayfinder Method four stages (Find Your Bearings, Wake Up the Inner Navigator, Draw Your Other Map, Stormproof Your Vessel) and similar recurring UI moments. Same Harbor Light palette, warm cream paper ground.

Reference samples (ChatGPT-generated, 2026-11-01): a brass compass rose on cream with navy/copper linework and restrained blue ink-splatter — closest hit on style; a sailboat-at-sunset watercolor scene — closest hit on the hero/photoreal-leaning tier once rebalanced onto warm cream instead of icy white.
