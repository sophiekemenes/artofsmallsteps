# RECHART email headers: image prompts (ChatGPT)

- **Status:** concepts ready for all emails up to lesson 11, 2026-11-20. Sophie generates them in ChatGPT.
- **Tool decision (Sophie, 2026-11-20):** ChatGPT. Canva was tested (welcome v1/v2, lesson 1 v1) and uses far too deep, strong colours.
- **Style source:** `../VISUAL-DIRECTION.md` (Harbor Light illustration tier) + the landing page images in `../landing-images/`.
- **How to use:** new ChatGPT message → attach the two reference images → paste the STYLE block → paste the SCENE of the email underneath.
- **Reference images:** `../landing-images/travel-trunk-map-boots.png` and `../landing-images/boarding-sailboat-from-pier.png`
- **Format:** 2:1 wide. In the email: 600 px wide (upload at 1200 px).
- **Rules:** no text in the image; nobody's face (a figure seen from behind is fine); never a cube, a ladder, a lion or a horse, and no single "the house" picture (Find Your Bearings symbols).
- **If ChatGPT overdoes it:** "Less paper grain and noise, keep the soft dusty washes." / "Softer, lighter colours, more cream paper showing."

## STYLE (paste first, every time)

```
Match the painting style of the two attached reference images: dusty, softly faded watercolour with mottled, slightly blotchy washes and smudged blooms, a little dry-brush texture, fine sketchy ink linework that is broken and lightly smudged, warm sepia and copper underwash showing through the blues. Paper grain subtle and fine, not heavy or noisy. Soft, light, airy colours with plenty of warm cream paper showing; never deep, saturated or digital. Palette: dusty blues (#1C4F6B, #7FA8C9), warm copper and sepia (#B87345), navy ink (#142230), warm cream paper (#F7F5F0). Wide 2:1 email header. Loose watercolour edges fading into the cream paper on the left and right, a few blue ink splatters, generous empty cream space at the top. No text, no logos, no watermark, no faces, no ladders, no lions, no horses.
```

## SCENES

### Prep 01 · Welcome: "You're on board"
```
Scene: a calm harbour in soft early-morning light. A small wooden sailboat is moored at the end of a short weathered wooden jetty, sail loosely furled, a coil of rope and a worn canvas travel bag waiting on the planks beside it, as if the boat is ready and waiting for its passenger. Gentle water with a few ripples, distant low blue hills, a pale sky with a faint warm glow on the horizon. Boat and jetty slightly right of centre.
```

### Prep 02 · Find Your Bearings: "Five quiet minutes and a pen"
```
Scene: first light on a quiet, misty shore. On a weathered wooden bench at the end of a jetty: an open notebook, a pencil resting on it, a small brass sextant beside it, and a steaming cup. The sea is still and pale, the mist just lifting. A moment of calm before anything begins. Objects slightly left of centre, lots of soft misty space.
```

### Prep 03 · Short prep: "We set off on Sunday" (just in time)
```
Scene: a small sailboat at a jetty in golden late-afternoon light, the sail just being raised and the mooring rope half loosened from the bollard. A traveller seen only from behind, unhurried, steps calmly onto the boat with a canvas bag over one shoulder, exactly at the right moment. Relaxed, good-humoured timing, nobody is rushing. Boat right of centre.
```

### Prep 04 · 6 December: "Good child or naughty child?"
```
Scene: a frosty winter morning seen through a round ship's porthole window. On the wooden sill inside: a pair of well-worn boots, one filled with tangerines, walnuts and small wrapped chocolates, and next to them a small bundle of golden birch twigs tied with a red ribbon. Soft snowflakes and a pale harbour outside the glass. Cosy, playful, a little mischievous. No Santa figure.
```

### Prep 05 · 20 December: "The most honest lie of December"
```
Scene: a ship's chart table on a winter evening. A small brass ship's clock, an hourglass with the sand half run through, a mug of tea, a sprig of pine, and one blank yellow sticky note stuck to the edge of an open notebook, a pencil beside it. Warm lamplight on the left, a dark blue porthole with a few snowflakes on the right. Quiet, thoughtful.
```

### Prep 06 · 1 January: "Sunday we set off. Here's what to pack."
```
Scene: New Year's dawn. A small sailboat leaving a quiet harbour, its sail just filling with wind, a soft trail of wake behind it. On the deck near the stern, small and simple: a notebook and a pen. Pale gold sunrise on the horizon, calm sea, the harbour wall and its little light falling behind. Hopeful, light, a beginning. Boat left of centre heading right.
```

### Lesson 1 · The Downstairs Neighbour
```
Scene: a small wooden sailboat floating on calm water at dusk, drawn as a gentle cutaway so we can see both above and below the deck. Above deck: quiet, simple, a furled sail, a coiled rope, an empty deck in soft evening light. Below deck, under the waterline, a cosy little cabin glowing warmly: a small brass lantern hanging from a beam casting copper-gold light, a hammock, a shelf of old books, rolled-up maps, an open notebook, a wooden chest with its lid slightly open. The cabin feels alive and busy, as if someone lives down there, though nobody is visible. The glowing space below is the heart of the picture. Boat centred.
```

### Lesson 2 · The Protagonist (same photo, three filters)
```
Scene: the inside wall of a ship's cabin with three round brass portholes side by side. Through each porthole the same view of the same sea and the same small island, but in three different moods: the first warm and golden like sunrise, the second misty blue and dreamy, the third grey-silver and moody. Below the portholes, on a small shelf, an old film camera rests. Gentle, curious, slightly playful.
```

### Lesson 3 · Added Value (a pen, a goblin and a joy detector)
```
Scene: close-up on a wooden desk by a porthole in soft morning light. A beautiful fountain pen with a copper nib lies across an open notebook, a few handwritten lines (illegible, no readable words) on the page, and a faint warm glow around the pen as if it were quietly special. A small brass compass and a cup nearby. Intimate, calm, a little magical but understated.
```

### Lesson 4 · Three Magic Words ("have to", "try", "problem": heavy luggage)
```
Scene: a small sailboat pulling away from a jetty, sitting high and light in the water. Left behind on the jetty planks: three heavy old canvas sacks tied with rope, sagging under their own weight. The boat's sail fills, a little spray at the bow, a sense of relief and lightness. Jetty and sacks on the left, boat moving away to the right.
```

### Lesson 5 · Wish List (free writing, a list with no limits, a faulty genie)
```
Scene: an open wooden sea chest on a ship's deck, a very long paper scroll spilling out of it and unrolling across the planks, covered in loose handwritten scribbles (illegible, no readable words). Beside it, a small brass oil lamp with a wisp of smoke coming out of the spout that curls up a little crookedly, as if the genie inside is not quite working properly. Playful, generous, slightly comic.
```

### Lesson 6 · Finger Exercise (a starry night, a secret in your hand)
```
Scene: a calm sea at night under a sky full of stars, a small sailboat far off on the horizon. In the foreground, an open hand seen from the side, palm up, thumb and fingertips gently touching, with one tiny warm star of copper-gold light resting at the fingertips. Deep but soft night blues fading into cream at the edges. Quiet, tender, a little magical.
```

### 6b · End of Stage 1: "Your navigator is awake. Now we draw."
```
Scene: a ship's chart table seen from slightly above. A big blank sheet of paper unrolled across it, corners held down by a brass compass and a small stone. Around it: a handful of coloured pencils, a few plain envelopes, an oil lamp casting warm light, and a porthole showing a calm morning sea. Everything ready, nothing drawn yet. Inviting, full of possibility.
```

### Lesson 7 · The Compass
```
Scene: on a sheet of cream paper on a wooden table, a hand-painted compass rose with exactly FIVE points, the point facing up the largest, each point coloured in to a different degree (one full, one half, one a thin sliver). Around it, five small paper cards fanned out (blank, no readable words) and a plain envelope. A couple of coloured pencils at the edge. Clear, simple, the compass rose is the hero.
```

### Lesson 8 · Home Port
```
Scene: a small, cosy harbour at early evening. A little sailboat moored at a wooden jetty, five small lanterns glowing along the jetty, warm lights in a few windows along the waterfront (several buildings, no single house in focus), smoke curling from a chimney, calm water reflecting the lights. A feeling of coming home, safety and warmth. Harbour centred.
```

### Lesson 9 · The Lighthouse
```
Scene: a tall striped lighthouse on a rocky point at dusk, its soft copper-gold beams fanning out across a wide, calm sea. Far away on the water, a very small sailboat, heading towards the light. Pale sky turning from cream to soft blue, the first stars. Hopeful, steady, a sense of direction. Lighthouse on the right, boat small on the left.
```

### Lesson 10 · Islands
```
Scene: a gentle bird's-eye view of a calm blue sea with five or six small islands of different shapes and sizes. Some islands are lush and fully coloured with soft greens and sand, others are pale, barely painted, almost just outlines; one or two have dotted coastlines like on a hand-drawn map. A tiny sailboat between them. The picture feels halfway between a real seascape and a map coming to life.
```

### Lesson 11 · X Marks the Spot
```
Scene: a finished hand-drawn map on a wooden table: a sea with several small islands, a compass rose in one corner, a striped lighthouse at the top edge, a small harbour in the middle, and on one island a big bold copper X. A few coloured pencils and a fountain pen lie beside it, one corner of the map slightly curling up. No readable words on the map. Satisfying, complete, a treasure-map feeling.
```
