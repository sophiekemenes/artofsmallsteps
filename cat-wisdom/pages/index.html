Once upon a time...

There was a street near the centre of Székesfehérvár, Hungary, a shop unit, and a shop window. Nothing remarkable ever happened in it.

Then one day a cat appeared in it. Her name was Rozi.

Soon after, Totó joined her, and from then on life on that street was never the same.

People stopped, smiled, and some even knocked on the glass. We would rather skip that last part.

Somewhere in the swirl of life, a question came up: what can two cats do to earn their daily treats? Could they actually get paid for it?

So after a short family council we decided to find out what happens when we put together what we are each good at. Me, my creativity. Them, competitive napping. Let the experiment begin!

Why?

Totó came to me during Covid, from the unsocialised cat gang of the Segítő Kéz Foundation in Tatabánya. Rozi joined him in November 2024. Until then she had lived in a cellar, with a dachshund for a friend. They are the kind of cats nobody lined up to adopt. They ended up with me.

They still hate being picked up. Not exactly social butterflies. But since the shop window became our life, something has changed.

Lying in a shop window has an interesting side effect: people stop. They smile. Sometimes they knock on the glass (let's say we'd rather skip that), but the smiles and the whole experience we love.

Since our little family has had no steady income for almost a year, we concluded the four-legged ones should try to contribute to the family budget too, because the Royal Canin Urinary won't buy itself.

And so the experiment began: what happens when a person puts even her most absurd resource to work for the cause?
Mine is creativity. Theirs is lying in the shop window.

After all, what could possibly go wrong?

Be part of the story!

Heads up: this is not a save-us campaign. Financial uncertainty or not, the cats will be fine, and that is non-negotiable. But there have been (and will be) times when I was the one giving, and I also know when it's time to ask.

So if you smiled, and chipping in won't leave you short, join the tuna sponsorship!

€1 is perfectly fine. It might be the price of a coffee, or a slice of pizza, or simply this: “These idiots made me laugh today, here you go.” That is reason enough for us.

You don't have to write anything. No note, no explanation, no introduction needed. The amount itself is the message. Pick a number, send it, and Rozi and Totó will translate the rest. If you do add a few words, we'll gladly pass them on.
