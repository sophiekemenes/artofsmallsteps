# "Újratervezés" ebook — source content analysis

Analysis of `ebook_text.txt` / `ut_work_ebook_0.pdf`, the compiled 15-lesson Hungarian email training that is the core source content for the Art of Small Steps global English adaptation.

---

## 1. Overall title, framing, purpose

- **Title**: "Újratervezés" ("Re-planning" / "Re-design" — redesigning one's life plan).
- **Author**: Kemenes Andrea Sophie ("Sophie"), described as running a "playful, yet completely serious, holistic goal-setting travel agency." Co-contributor: **Szűcs Tibor** (founder of "Mindennapi NLP" / celkituzotanfolyam.hu), who authors the "Planning phase" lessons.
- **Brand**: Published under kincsesterkep.hu ("Treasure Map"). The brand is thematically load-bearing, not just a company name: the subconscious is repeatedly called a "kincsesbánya" (treasure mine) / "kincsesláda" (treasure chest) whose keys the reader collects lesson by lesson — the whole training is framed as a treasure hunt inside oneself.
- **Purpose**: explicitly NOT a technique-dump. "A tréning rólad fog szólni. Rólad, végig." ("This training will be about YOU. About you, all the way through.") — self-discovery, values clarification, building a personalized, doable action plan.
- **Three-part declared structure**:
  1. **Preparation/Warm-up** — how you work, hidden abilities, the subconscious
  2. **Planning phase** (Szűcs Tibor) — mapping values + plan-making
  3. **Action phase** (Sophie) — solutions for pitfalls/challenges, motivation
- Explicitly reusable: "a training you can pick back up any time you need a reminder."

## 3. Recurring concepts / frameworks

- **Treasure Map brand tie-in**: subconscious = treasure mine/chest, lessons = keys. Central metaphor to redesign for the new brand.
- **"The Downstairs Neighbor"** = subconscious, personified as a childlike, symbol-loving housemate (deliberate non-cliché alternative to "iceberg").
- **The Pen** and **The Notebook** — physical ritual objects/tools used throughout.
- **Envelopes** and **colored index cards** — recurring tactile devices for values, doubts, roles, goals.
- **NLP toolkit**: submodalities (L2, L8, L10, L14), anchoring (L3, L6, L14), reframing (L4), two presuppositions (L15).
- **Core cascading methodology**: Values (card-sort) → Roles → Goals → "Ten Things to Achieve Your Goal" checklist (Milestones → Tasks → Habits).
- Whimsical, non-literal wordplay titles throughout — needs equivalent inventive (not literal) English wordplay.

## 4. Voice

Warm, playful, second-person direct address, confessional personal anecdotes, liberal emoticons, self-aware humor, pop-culture shorthand (Star Wars, Yoda, Elizabeth Gilbert, Amy Cuddy, Neil Gaiman), two distinct author voices (Sophie = narrative/story-driven; Szűcs Tibor = structured/checklist-driven, dry humor). Not a terse corporate-coach voice.

Representative lines (HU → EN):
- "A tréning rólad fog szólni. Rólad, végig." → "This training will be about you. About you, all the way through."
- "A dolog ugyanaz. A kép ugyanaz. Semmi nem változott. Csak a filter." → "The thing is the same. The picture is the same. Nothing changed. Only the filter."
- "Nincs kudarc, csak visszajelzés." → "There is no failure, only feedback."
- "Az ember nem egyenlő a viselkedésével." → "A person is not equal to their behavior."

## 5. Hungary-specific elements needing adaptation (not translation)

- Currency (Ft → globally-relevant currency/round numbers)
- Local brands/services: UPC (ISP), BKV (Budapest transit), Kapucíner/Fornetti (confectionery brands)
- Local geography: Vác lake house anecdote
- Hungarian folk sayings ("only a dead fish swims with the current", the cruder wind idiom) — need transcreation, not literal translation
- Neil Gaiman and Elizabeth Gilbert quotes are back-translated from English in the HU version — **source the original English quotes directly** for the English edition
- Pop-culture references (Star Wars, TED talks) are already global and can stay
